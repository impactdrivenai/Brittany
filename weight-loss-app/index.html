My Protocol · Quick Reference
EVERY MEAL — Protein first (30g+ · eggs, chicken, red meat, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, edamame, whey), non-starchy veggies second, carbs last. 8–10 plant servings/day. Legumes when it works. Ground flax + chia daily for omega-3s. Olive oil is your friend.
SUGAR STRATEGY — One planned "designated sweet" per day, always paired with protein + fat (dark chocolate + almonds, berries + Greek yogurt). Never eat sugar alone. As insulin resistance improves, cravings drop 60–80%.
AFTER EVERY MEAL — 10–15 min walk. Drops your glucose spike ~30%. Non-negotiable.
WEEKLY TRAINING (PHASE 1) — 3× strength (compound lifts · your power-athlete superpower) + 1× easy zone-2 run/week (keeping the ultra dream warm) + daily post-meal walks. Phase 2 adds more running after 30–40 lbs down.
GLUCOSE TARGETS — Fasting <95, post-meal (2hr) <140, A1C <5.6%. Reversing insulin resistance is the lever that moves everything.
SLEEP 7.5+ HRS — Dark, cool room. No phone in bed. Poor sleep = insulin resistance next day. Ask your doc about a CGM.
DAILY SUPPLEMENT STACK — Metformin, methyl-B12, inositol 40:1, D3+K2, magnesium PM, algae omega-3. Optional berberine (ask doc first).
NOT MEDICAL ADVICE — Run your protocol by your endocrinologist. Especially berberine + metformin, supplement changes, and glucose ranges specific to your case.
