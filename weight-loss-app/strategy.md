## Nutrition Framework (Since Plant-Based Wasn't Sustainable)

Plant-based worked because it was accidentally **low glycemic + high fiber + anti-inflammatory** — not because it was plant-based. We keep those three things, we drop the all-or-nothing constraint.

### The framework: Plant-Forward Mediterranean

**Daily target (also loaded as defaults in the app):**
- **1600 calories**
- **150g protein** — muscle retention, satiety, glucose stability (your power-athlete genetics love this)
- **100g carbs** — from beans/lentils/berries/veggies/some starchy tubers, not from bread/pasta/rice at first
- **35g fiber** — the #1 lever for glucose control most people ignore
- **70g fat** — olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish

### Meal template (every meal)

1. **Protein first** — 30g+ per meal. Chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lentils, tempeh, tofu, protein shake as backup.
2. **Non-starchy veggies second** — half the plate.
3. **Carbs last** — this order alone drops glucose spikes ~30%.

### Foods to lean into (evidence-backed for YOUR labs)

- **Legumes daily** (lentils, black beans, chickpeas) — high fiber + protein + resistant starch = massive glucose benefit
- **Fatty fish 2–3×/week** (salmon, sardines) — omega-3s reduce inflammation and improve insulin sensitivity
- **Berries daily** — highest-fiber lowest-glycemic fruits
- **Cruciferous veggies** (broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, brussels sprouts) — support estrogen metabolism, help with androgen excess
- **Extra virgin olive oil** — 1–2 tbsp/day. Anti-inflammatory. Also the Mediterranean diet MVP.
- **Nuts & seeds** — walnuts, almonds, chia, flax. Fiber + healthy fats.
- **Cinnamon** in your coffee/oatmeal — modest insulin-sensitivity boost.
- **Fermented foods** — kimchi, sauerkraut, plain Greek yogurt. Gut health = insulin sensitivity.

### Foods to be strategic about

- **Refined carbs, bread, pasta, rice, cereal, most crackers** — spike you. Not banned, but time them around workouts and limit to occasional.
- **Fruit juice, smoothies with lots of fruit, sweetened yogurt** — sneaky sugar bombs.
- **"Diet" foods with sugar alcohols** — can wreck your gut and cravings.
- **Alcohol** — hits insulin, sleep, and estrogen metabolism. If you drink, dry wine over sweet, occasional.

### The 3 rules if you forget everything else

1. **Protein first, veggies second, carbs last.**
2. **Walk 10–15 min after every meal.**
3. **Sleep 7.5+ hours in a cool, dark room.**

## Training Framework

Your 23andMe literally says "elite power athlete muscle composition." Your body was built for **strength and power**, not marathon cardio.

### Weekly template

- **3–4× strength training** (45–60 min each)
  - Compound lifts: squat, deadlift, bench, overhead press, row, hip thrust
  - Progressive overload: add weight or reps weekly
  - Rep range: 5–12 for hypertrophy + strength
- **Daily walks after meals** — this is your cardio. 30–45 min of walking/day, split up. Drops glucose spikes better than 30 min at once.
- **1–2× "fun" cardio** — dancing, hiking, bike, skating — whatever's fun for your ADHD brain. Optional.
- **Zone 2 (optional)** — 1× 30-min steady-state at conversational pace helps mitochondria & IR long-term.

### Why not "more cardio"?

- Strength preserves muscle during weight loss (critical — muscle is your glucose sink)
- Muscle mass = more insulin sensitivity 24/7
- Your genetics favor it
- Less time (huge — you have a kid + a business)
- Lower cortisol than long cardio (helps hormones)

**Start where you are.** Week 1–2: two 30-min sessions with bodyweight or light dumbbells. Build from there.

## Supplement Stack (Ask Your Endocrinologist Before Starting)

Loaded into the app under Settings → Medications. Check these off daily.

| Supplement | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Metformin** (as prescribed) | First-line insulin sensitizer | Take with food. Depletes B12. |
| **Myo-inositol + D-chiro (40:1)** | Insulin sensitizer, huge for PCOS-like androgen excess. ~2g myo-inositol + 50mg D-chiro, twice daily. | Often noticeable within 6–8 weeks |
| **Methylcobalamin B12** | Metformin depletes B12. Your MTHFR variant means take methylated form. 1000mcg/day. | Non-negotiable while on metformin |
| **Vitamin D3 + K2** | ~5000 IU D3 + 100mcg K2/day (test level first — most IR women are deficient) | Insulin sensitivity, mood, immunity |
| **Magnesium glycinate** | 300–400mg at night. Improves insulin sensitivity + sleep. | Glycinate = gentle, doesn't cause GI issues |
| **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA 2g)** | Anti-inflammatory. Improves IR. | Look for 3rd-party tested (Nordic Naturals, Thorne) |
| **Berberine (optional)** | "Nature's metformin" — 500mg 3× daily with meals. Powerful glucose-lowering. | ⚠️ Don't stack with metformin without doctor OK — can drop glucose too much |
| **Creatine (optional)** | 5g/day. Strength training performance, brain health. | Safe, well-studied. Great combo with your power-athlete profile |

## Monitoring Cadence (log in the app under Progress → Labs)

- **Daily**: weight, fasting glucose (if you have a meter), food, water, movement, sleep, mood
- **Weekly**: progress photo (same outfit, same light)
- **Monthly**: measurements — waist, hips, thigh, arm
- **Every 3 months**: A1C, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HOMA-IR
- **Every 6 months**: full hormone panel — total T, free T, SHBG, estradiol, progesterone, DHEA-S. B12, vitamin D, hs-CRP, lipids, liver panel.
- **Ask your doctor about a Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)** — you can get one via prescription or now direct-to-consumer (Stelo, Lingo, Nutrisense). This is the biggest missing piece for most T2D people. It shows YOU which foods spike YOU.

## Red Flags — Escalate to Your Doctor

- Fasting glucose over 140 for 3+ days in a row
- Any glucose reading over 200
- New symptoms: severe fatigue, blurry vision, thirst spike, frequent urination
- Rapid hair changes (loss or growth) beyond baseline
- Menstrual cycle disruption
- Chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness
