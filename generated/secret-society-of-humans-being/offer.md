# Offer Strategy — The Secret Society of Humans Being

## Offer Name
**The Secret Society of Humans Being**

## One-Liner
A phone-free, AI-free secret society that meets in dark, beautiful rooms so you can remember what it feels like to be a person.

## Price Point

### Founding Membership: $33/month (or $297/year)
**Reasoning:**
- $33/month is low enough to be a no-brainer impulse ("less than a night out") but high enough to create commitment — people who pay show up
- The annual option ($297) gives a ~25% discount and locks in founding members before the price rises
- The number 33 feels intentional, not corporate. It's not $29.99. It's not $49. It's $33 — because this isn't a subscription, it's a membership to something that matters
- As chapters open in more cities, price increases to $55/month for new members

## What's Included

- **Monthly gathering** in your city's chapter — held in a curated dark, intimate venue (speakeasy bars, candlelit back rooms, brick-walled basements, fireside lounges)
- **The evening's ritual**: Each gathering has a theme and an activity — group reading, poetry sharing, letter writing, drawing, storytelling, uncomfortable silence, collaborative art, or just... being
- **Phone lockbox at the door** — your phone goes in a velvet pouch when you walk in. You get it back when you leave. Non-negotiable.
- **The Society Scroll** — a printed, mailed quarterly zine featuring member-submitted art, writing, mistakes, confessions, and bad poetry. No PDFs. No links. Paper and ink.
- **Access to the Members' Directory** — a physical (yes, printed) directory of members in your city for organizing informal meetups between gatherings
- **Founding Member status** — your name on the Wall of Humans (a physical wall in each chapter venue). First 100 members per city only.
- **Chapter expansion voting rights** — founding members vote on which city opens next

## Bonuses

### Bonus 1: The First Gathering Kit (Value: $45)
Mailed to you before your first gathering. Includes:
- A hand-stamped wax-sealed invitation to your first meeting
- A pocket-sized notebook (unlined — because rules are for apps)
- A single match and a tea candle — "Light this when you need to remember"
- A card with the Society's only rule: *"Be here. Be bad at it. Be human."*

### Bonus 2: The Mistake Museum Submission (Value: Priceless)
Every member gets to submit one "beautiful mistake" — a failed poem, a terrible drawing, a confession, an embarrassing story — to be displayed anonymously at gatherings. Because the things we hide are the most human things about us.

### Bonus 3: Founding Member Wax Seal Stamp (Value: $35)
A custom brass wax seal with the Society's emblem. For sealing letters, journals, or just feeling like you belong to something older than the internet.

## Guarantee

**The First Gathering Guarantee:**
Come to your first gathering. If you don't leave feeling more alive than you have in months — if you don't feel something shift — email us and we'll refund your membership in full. No forms. No "feedback surveys." Just an honest email. We'll believe you.

We can guarantee this because the gatherings work. Phones down. Candles lit. Humans being. It works every time.

## Urgency / Scarcity Mechanism

**Founding membership is capped at 100 members per city.**

This isn't artificial scarcity — the rooms we meet in hold 30-50 people. When a chapter fills, the waitlist opens. Founding members lock in the $33/month rate permanently. Everyone after pays $55.
