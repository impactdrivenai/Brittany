## Email 2: Day 2 (Momentum + Support)

**Subject line:** Day two. How was the quiet?
**Alt subject 1:** A small thing to try this week.
**Alt subject 2:** You're not crazy for joining. Here's why.

---

Hey —

Quick check-in. Did you try it last night? The candle, the quiet, the 10 minutes?

If you did — how did it feel? Weird? Boring? Surprisingly emotional? All of those are the right answer.

If you didn't — no guilt. Tonight works too. So does tomorrow. This isn't a program with a Day 1 and a Day 2. There's no falling behind. There's just the next time you choose to be present.

**One thing you might be feeling right now:**

You might be wondering if joining a "secret society" was impulsive. It wasn't.

Impulsive is scrolling for 45 minutes before bed and not remembering a single thing you saw. Impulsive is saying yes to plans you don't want because you saw someone else's weekend on Instagram.

Joining a room full of strangers who all agreed to put their phones away and look each other in the eye? That's one of the most deliberate things you've done in a while. You know it. That's why you're here.

**Something to try this week:**

Write a letter by hand. To someone you love. Not a text. Not a DM. A letter.

It doesn't have to be long. A few lines. "I was thinking about you. Here's why." Put it in an envelope. Mail it. Or hand it to them. Watch what happens to their face.

This is what the Society is about — not grand gestures, but the small human ones we stopped doing because a screen was always closer.
